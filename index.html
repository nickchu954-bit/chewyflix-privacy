ChewyFlix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ChewyFlix is an independent app for watching the movies and TV shows on a private Jellyfin media server run by Nick Chu. It is not made by or affiliated with the Jellyfin project. This policy explains what ChewyFlix stores and where.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. ChewyFlix doesn’t sell or share your data.
  • ChewyFlix talks to two places only: Nick’s media server, and Tailscale’s service, which sets up the private, encrypted connection to that server.

What stays on your device

A sign-in key from the server (never your password), this phone’s key for the private connection, your settings and, in sample mode, the pretend viewing history. Deleting the app removes all of this.

What’s stored on the media server

The server keeps your account and, as Jellyfin always does, what you’ve watched, where you stopped in each film or episode, and your favorites, so you can pick up where you left off.

If you ask for an account in ChewyFlix (or ChewyHub), the server also stores your first and last name, email address and (optionally) phone number. They are used only so the server’s owner can approve your account and so you can reset your password by email. Only the server’s owner can see them.

The private connection (Tailscale)

When you sign in, ChewyFlix joins the server’s private network using Tailscale, a service from Tailscale Inc. To set up the connection, Tailscale’s service receives this phone’s name on that network (which includes your username), its network addresses and its operating system. Your films and shows travel encrypted between your phone and the server; Tailscale can’t see them. Signing out disconnects this phone, and deleting your account removes it from the network. Tailscale’s own privacy policy is at tailscale.com/privacy-policy.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and the personal details stored with it in Settings → Delete my account. This deletes the same account in ChewyHub.
  • Edit your name, email and phone in the ChewyHub app (Settings → Account → Your profile), or ask the server’s owner.

Children

ChewyFlix isn’t directed at children under 13, and its sign-up isn’t intended for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
